## What is PromptNerdy?

PromptNerdy is an open source SEO platform for business owners and marketers. Our focus is to make SEO simple and help you use AI to grow your business.

That means useful data, help deciding what to do next, and tools that work with the AI agents you already use. You can research keywords, understand competitors and backlinks, improve your site, and plan content around your business goals.

## Who is PromptNerdy for?

<h3 id="entrepreneurs">Entrepreneurs doing SEO for their own business</h3>

You want more customers to find your website. You need a way to work out what matters, take action, and get help when you're stuck, at a price that makes sense for your business.

- Work with a familiar AI agent that can explain findings and help you choose a next step.
- Get guidance through [SEO coaching](/docs/skills/seo-coach) and focused skills, without learning a large SEO suite first.
- Keep your business background and previous research available as you work on your site over time.

<h3 id="marketers">Marketers building their AI workflow</h3>

You know SEO and have your own approach. PromptNerdy gives you a foundation for working with AI across projects, so you can spend more time on your clients and less time building an internal dashboard.

- Keep each project's goals, positioning, and reports available to your team and connected agents.
- Adapt the skills to your methods and create report templates for the work you repeat.
- Control your research and tracking spend, and customize the open source platform when you need to.

## What makes PromptNerdy different?

We build PromptNerdy for you and your AI agent to work together. To us, being AI native means giving your agent access to the data and project work it needs to be useful over time.

- Work with your choice of compatible agent, such as Claude Code, Codex, or [ChatGPT with a configured connection](/docs/chatgpt). [MCP](/features/mcp) lets it research SEO data and read and save project work in PromptNerdy.
- Get advice that reflects your business. Each project stores your business overview, current goal, positioning, and writing preferences. Your agent can reference details such as “we sell to independent retailers” or “avoid jargon in our copy.”
- Give your agent a process to follow. [PromptNerdy skills](/docs/skills) guide keyword research, competitor analysis, link prospecting, local SEO, and audits. They help turn the data into recommendations you can act on.
- Keep useful work accessible. Agents can save reports in PromptNerdy for you and your teammates to read, then consult them when you return to the project. Reports can be shaped around the question you're answering.

## A platform you can make your own

Your workflow can evolve as you learn what works. In an agent that supports custom skills, you can ask it to turn your own SEO process into a reusable skill. PromptNerdy supplies the data and shared project context that skill can use.

The software is [open source](/open-source-seo), so you can inspect it, adapt it, or self-host it with your own provider accounts.

Hosted PromptNerdy starts at $10 a month, including $10 of usage credits. You can try it before subscribing and buy more credits as your needs grow. Self-hosted users cover their own hosting and data costs. [See pricing](/pricing).
